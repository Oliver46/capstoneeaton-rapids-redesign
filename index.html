Insurance & payment

Is my insurance accepted?

Health plans can change their networks from year to year. Please call us at 517-836-2178 to confirm your coverage before your visit.

We work with

Accepted insurance

  • BCBS / BCN
  • Medicare
  • Aetna (most plans)
  • Humana (most plans)
  • Priority Health
  • Cigna (network allowing)
  • Multiplan
  • TRICARE (TRICARE-certified out-of-network provider)
  • UofM HP / PHP
  • Most TPAs (Allied, IAS, Unified, etc.)

Not sure about your plan? Call 517-836-2178 to confirm your coverage.

We don’t work with

Not accepted

  • Medicaid plans, primary or secondary
  • Health Alliance Plan (HAP)
  • McLaren
  • Meridian
  • New auto insurance or workers’ compensation cases (not accepted at this time)

This is not a complete list. Some plans change from year to year.

Why don’t you accept Medicaid?

We are unable to accept or bill Medicaid plans because of a longstanding credentialing limitation, and this is unlikely to change.

Payment

Paying for your own care?

We offer self-pay options. Please ask at the front desk.

Self-pay is not available for Medicaid-covered services, per CMS guidelines and federal law.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about hours, insurance and visits. Still have a question? Call 517-836-2178.

What are your office hours?

Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (closed 12:00 to 1:00 PM for lunch), and Friday, 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. We are closed Saturday and Sunday. Holiday hours may differ, so please call to confirm. See the full schedule.

Can I be seen the same day if I’m sick?

We offer same-day treatment options when you’re sick. Call the office at 517-836-2178 to ask about availability.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We accept BCBS/BCN, Medicare, most Aetna and Humana plans, Priority Health, Cigna (network allowing), Multiplan, TRICARE (as a TRICARE-certified out-of-network provider), UofM HP/PHP and most TPAs. Plans change from year to year, so please call to confirm your coverage. See the insurance list.

Do you accept Medicaid?

No. We are unable to accept or bill Medicaid plans, as primary or secondary insurance, because of a longstanding credentialing limitation.

Can I pay for my visit myself?

Yes, we offer self-pay options. Please ask at the front desk. Self-pay is not available for Medicaid-covered services, per CMS guidelines and federal law.

Who will I see?

Pam Becker, NP-C, a board-certified Family Practice Nurse Practitioner with a Master of Science in Nursing from Michigan State University.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
